\begin{abstract}
This report evaluates recorded behavior through reusable, acquisition-aware requirement monitors.
Simulator adapters supply independent geometry and actual consumer/actuator timing.
Missing evidence remains inconclusive; unmeasured requirements remain unavailable.
The results are finite sampled-trace evidence, not continuous safety or discrete supervisor proofs.
\end{abstract}
\section{System and requirements}
The system uses ROS 2 Humble, PX4 SITL, and Gazebo Harmonic in Linux.
The UAV supplies independent camera observations; the UGV consumes a fused map pose and guarded routes.
Truth is confined to sensor synthesis and scoring. Positions use ENU map coordinates and body data use FLU.
The verification library consumes normalized numerical traces independently of ROS and Gazebo.

R1 checks sampled planar disk clearance against static boxes/cylinders.
R2 checks arrival within the declared deadline, with estimated and physical tolerances and a quiet dwell.
R3 checks bounded response to an actual invalid guard, followed by a sustained zero command until recovery.
R4 separates actuator-output expiry from independently measured physical settling.
R6 checks acquisition-matched position error conditional on a valid pose and fresh UAV input.
Availability follows the exported profiles and capability table. Planned exclusions are ${exclusions_tex}.
This trace report does not assert a supervisor model-checking result.
\paragraph{Recorded-trace conditions.}
Let \(A\) denote a confirmed arrival dwell inside both tolerances with quiet commands;
\(I,Q,V\) denote invalid guard, quiet command, and valid guard; and \(E\) denote fresh UAV input with valid pose.
\[
\begin{array}{ll}
R1:&G_{\rm valid}(d_{\rm clear}\geq-\delta_C)\\
R2:&F_{[0,D_A]}A\\
R3:&G(I\rightarrow F_{[0,D_G]}(Q\land(Q\ W\ V)))\\
R6:&G(E\rightarrow e_{\rm matched}\leq\epsilon_L)
\end{array}
\]
Weak until \(W\) permits a quiet hold to continue to recording end without requiring recovery.
R4 bounds actuator-output expiry and independently measured physical settling with a confirming dwell.
The profile supplies the arrival deadline $$D_A$$, guard deadline $$D_G$$, error bound $$\epsilon_L$$, dwell times, and all other numerical bounds; \texttt{bounds.csv} retains every evaluated parameter set.
Source profiles and saved parameters remain authoritative.
\section{Temporal semantics and instrumentation}
Integer nanosecond stamps retain clock domain and epoch. Acquisition, consumer receipt,
command publication, actuator receipt/output, and truth motion remain separate reference points.
Closed response windows include the deadline. Discrete samples expire at their maximum validity gap;
interpolation is confined to continuous pose scoring within declared matching gaps.
Missing coverage, untriggered obligations, and insufficient horizons remain inconclusive.
A witnessed violation is retained despite unrelated incomplete evidence.
R4 uses steady time and interval-average physical motion with sampling uncertainty.
A paused simulator cannot establish physical settling from repeated unchanged positions.

\section{Failure search and replay}
Search records ${search_trials} attempts and ${search_wall_s_formatted} measured driver wall seconds,
with ${replay_attempts} search-counterexample replays and ${diagnostic_replay_attempts} supplemental diagnostic replays.

The sole search objective is minimum sampled R1 clearance. Missing evidence has no objective value.
Generated worlds, offsets, seeds, commands, budgets, original failures, and replay links are retained.
Matching seeds need not reproduce identical feedback trajectories. A finite search without a
counterexample does not prove safety. Reproduced and non-reproduced outcomes remain separate.
Post-hoc diagnostic replays remain separate from the held-out matrix and original search budget.
\section{Claims, limits, and reproduction}
The claim/evidence table records every requirement's verdict counts and trigger/eligible coverage.
The results do not establish continuous collision freedom, online detector benefit, hazard response,
or a discrete supervisor proof. Incompatible source/profile/evaluator cohorts remain separate.
Calibration assumptions and evaluated source-run identities remain explicit. Raw recording availability is explicit.
Saved results and portable display series regenerate these tables and figures without simulation.
The live demo reuses launch, recording, evaluation, and cleanup; mandatory failure or incomplete
evidence prevents a successful verification summary. Recorded replay is labeled as replay.
