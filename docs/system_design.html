Prototype limits

  • One hovering UAV, one UGV, a roof tag, and known camera geometry.
  • Flat simulated ground. The camera goal-demo world has no physical obstacles.
  • Brightness classification supplies the current terrain grid. Semantic hazard recognition remains proposed.
  • The UAV retains an independent global reference during UGV GPS loss.
